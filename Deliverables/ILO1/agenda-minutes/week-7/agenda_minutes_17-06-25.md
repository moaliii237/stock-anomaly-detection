#### Agenda item 2: Project Retrospective

##### Key Discussions - agenda item 2

**What Went Well:**

- **Teamwork**: The team felt they integrated well and shared a similar vision. Dealing with a non-contributing member was seen as a realistic experience that brought the rest of the team closer.
- **Idea Generation**: The team's ideas were very similar from the start, allowing them to finalize their project concept in a single day.
- **Feature Engineering**: Hris was praised for quickly adapting the feature engineering when the team had to switch from Apple to P&G stock due to missing data. The features were considered good and helpful for the model.
- **Model Implementation**: The team successfully implemented different models, including an LSTM model, and achieved a "usable" result after hyperparameter tuning.

**What Didn't Go Well:**

- **Block Structure**: The project started too late (week 3 or 4), compressing a significant amount of work into the final week. It was noted the block was shortened from a planned 8 weeks to 7.
- **Initial Data Quality**: The first dataset had significant data gaps, an issue that was discovered late in week 5 because the person responsible had not communicated the problem.
- **UI Development**: Too much time was spent on UI tasks (HTML) instead of core data science work. This was exacerbated by trusting the task to a teammate who did not complete it, forcing a last-minute effort from others.
- **Model Performance**: Szymon expressed dissatisfaction with the final model, stating it was suitable for academia but not for a real-world environment. This was largely because it lacked external data like news, which is crucial for stock market prediction.
- **Hyperparameter Tuning Task**: The team was confused about how to approach the hyperparameter tuning task, as they felt they had already performed extensive tuning to arrive at their current model and creating another model didn't make sense.

**Improvements:**

- **Earlier Technical Training**: The team suggested that training on essential tools like Git, GitHub, and concepts like Poetry testing should be introduced in Week 1, not Week 6.
- **Better Teamwork Workshops**: The mandatory Lego teamwork workshop was seen as ineffective and restrictive. It was suggested that workshops should focus on practical teamwork skills like task division.

##### Decisions Made - agenda item 2

- The team agreed that the feedback on the block's structure and content should be formally submitted through feedback forms.
- The team decided to ask a teacher for clarification on the hyperparameter tuning task.
